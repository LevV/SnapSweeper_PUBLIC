# PhotoMoto__PUBLIC
Do you dump all your photos into a single folder on your drive?

Or are you a meticulous person who — like me — likes to keep your photo library neatly organized by date, camera, lens, or even (if desired) focal length?

If so, this app is for you.

Meticulously tested, the app takes your photos and places them into a destination folder, carefully sorting them into subfolders according to your selected folder schema. The app offers several predefined folder schemas, or allows you to create your own.

What’s more, you can detect duplicates between the source and destination folders. Alternatively, you can scan a single folder for duplicates and decide whether to keep or remove them. You can even search for “almost identical” photos, helping you find images that are very close to each other.

Needless to say, none of your photos are ever uploaded anywhere. No cloud, no snooping — 100% local.
